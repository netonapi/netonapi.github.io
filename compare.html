NetonAPI vs new-api / one-api ​

NetonAPI is not a fork of one-api / new-api. It was designed from the operator's side: prices are anchored to the official price, every channel carries its real cost, a price that would lose money cannot be saved, and every charge reconciles as "unit price × tokens". The table compares it with the two most widely used open-source relay projects.
